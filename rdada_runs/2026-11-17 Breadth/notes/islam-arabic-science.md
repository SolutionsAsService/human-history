# islam-arabic-science: Arabic script, early Qur'an, Medina, papyri, House of Wisdom, Indian Ocean trade

## Ask
Arabic script and language: the Namara inscription (328 CE), earliest Quranic manuscripts (Sana'a palimpsest, Birmingham fragment: radiocarbon and text claims), the Constitution of Medina, and Islamic conquest administration papyri. The House of Wisdom and the translation movement (sources beyond Gutas if open), Arab-Indian Ocean trade (Sulayman the Merchant, Akhbar al-Sin wa'l-Hind). Quote primary passages at their loci with composition date and basis, and note disputes (e.g. radiocarbon dating of the Birmingham fragment).

## Cited findings
- Namara: RES 483 prints Dussaud's reading, "en caractères nabatéens et en langue arabe", and the date "l'an 223, le septième jour de kesloûl" — [RES vol. 1](https://archive.org/stream/repertoiredepigr0001unse/repertoiredepigr0001unse_djvu.txt). The conversion to 7 Dec AD 328 and Bellamy's 1985 revised reading are on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namara_inscription) only; Bellamy's article was not opened.
- Constitution of Medina: clause 2 "one community (umma)" in [Guillaume's translation](https://constitution.org/1-Constitution/cons/medina/con_medina.htm); clause 28 and the authenticity argument in [Lecker 2004](https://archive.org/stream/the-constitution-of-medina-muhammads-first-legal-document-by-micheal-lecker-2004/The%20Constitution%20Of%20Medina%20Muhammads%20First%20Legal%20Document%20by%20Micheal%20Lecker%202004_djvu.txt). Lecker: original not preserved, widely acknowledged authentic, one unified document; he calls Serjeant's division into several documents unconvincing. Composition range 622-623 is my approximation of Lecker's "shortly after the hijra".
- Birmingham (Mingana 1572a): Oxford radiocarbon 568-645 CE at 95%+, and no palimpsest on multispectral/XRF per the owner, [University of Birmingham](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/news/2025/the-birmingham-quran-an-ancient-manuscript-forging-modern-day-connection). Contents are 18:17-31, 19:91-98, 20:1-40 per the [FAQ](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/faqs). Q 19:92 quoted from [Pickthall via alquran.cloud](https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/ayah/19:92/en.pickthall).
- San'a' 1: parchment of one folio before AD 671 (99%), before 646 (75%); suras formed before 'Uthman, in [Sadeghi and Goudarzi 2012](https://archive.org/stream/110978941Sanaa1AndTheOriginsOfTheQurAn/110978941-Sanaa-1-and-the-Origins-of-the-Qur-An_djvu.txt) (unofficial scan; DOI 10.1515/islam-2011-0025).
- Papyri: PERF 558, Arabic-Greek receipt of Jumada I 22 AH (25 Apr 643), translation on [Islamic Awareness](https://www.islamic-awareness.org/history/islam/papyri/perf558).
- Translation movement: al-Khwarizmi's preface credits al-Ma'mun's patronage ([Rosen 1831](https://archive.org/stream/algebraofmohamme00khuwuoft/algebraofmohamme00khuwuoft_djvu.txt)); a translation of al-Nadim's report of envoys including Salm, head of the Bayt al-Hikma, sent for Greek books ([erenow copy of a book](https://erenow.org/postclassical/abbasid-house-of-wisdom-between-myth-and-reality/8.php)); Indian astronomical tables (Sindhind) translated for al-Mansur in 772 ([Jaouiche, arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.0874)).
- Indian Ocean trade: Muslim judge at Canfu in [Renaudot 1733](https://archive.org/stream/ancientaccountso00sira/ancientaccountso00sira_djvu.txt); the first part is by an unknown mariner, the second by al-Sirafi fifty years later ([NYU Press](https://nyupress.org/9781479830596/accounts-of-china-and-india/)). Sulayman's date "around AD 850" is from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sulaiman_al-Tajir) only.

## Sceptical and alternative pass
- Birmingham dating: searched for critiques and found them. [Fedeli's own thesis](https://etheses.bham.ac.uk/id/eprint/5864/1/Fedeli15PhD.pdf) says text and script (second half of the first century AH) disagree with the 568-645 range, cites other manuscripts where radiocarbon and palaeography diverge, and advises dating a further sample. [Rezvan](https://www.kunstkamera.ru/images/news/2015/efimrezvan-lecture.pdf) says radiocarbon dates the animal and proposes 50-70 years of parchment storage. [Small (Bodleian)](https://digitalmushaf.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/2015/12/04/the-birmingham-quran-too-many-options/) withholds judgment. Deroche's reservations, Shoemaker, al-Sarhan and Shah are reported only in secondary pages (Wikipedia, press), so they are not candidates.
- Medina: Goldziher's fabrication view appears only as quoted by Lecker (candidate marked skeptical). Serjeant, Gil, Wellhausen not opened beyond Lecker's summaries. The source base for authenticity is thus Lecker-heavy.
- House of Wisdom: the erenow book argues against the maximalist "university" myth and cites Gutas's minimalist reading; Gutas himself was not opened.
- Indian influence: Jaouiche notes Pingree found Greek astronomy problems in the Sindhind fragments, which cuts against a purely Indian origin.
- Namara: Bellamy's reading differs from Dussaud's on the title; only the Wikipedia rendering was read.
