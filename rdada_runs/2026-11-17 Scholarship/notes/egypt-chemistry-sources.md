# egypt-chemistry-sources: Primary analytical sources for Khemet's chemical crafts

## Ask
Open the primary analytical papers behind Khemet's chemical crafts: Walter et al. 1999 (Nature), Rehren and Pusch 2005 (Science), Kovalev et al. 2023 (JAS), Hatton Shortland Tite 2008, Odler on copper, Buckley and Evershed 2001 and 2004 full text. Use open copies via Unpaywall/Crossref, repositories, or PMC. Quote findings and limits. Add any open text on Egyptian faience production and on gold metallurgy.

## Cited findings
All 15 candidates have quotes confirmed with check_quote.py (candidates file). Where only an abstract page was opened, that is stated.
- Cosmetics: laurionite and phosgenite in Egyptian cosmetic powders read as synthetic "wet chemistry" products (Walter 1999, abstract only) — [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/17240). A 2022 open study of 11 kohl containers still says "likely stems from synthetic manufacture" and finds manganese, silicon and organic ingredients too — [Sci Rep](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8994005/). Nubian cosmetics add synthetic calcium antimonate — [PLOS ONE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12425255/).
- Embalming: Buckley and Evershed 2001 and 2004 are open only as abstracts (White Rose), see Gaps. 2004 animal-mummy balms are as complex as human ones — [White Rose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/7100/). Full-text open: Saqqara workshop vessels (26th Dyn.) link antiu/sefet to oil, tar and resin mixtures with imported ingredients — [Nature 2023 via PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9908542/); KV42 balms of c. 1450 BCE — [Sci Rep](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10471619/).
- Egyptian blue: New Kingdom shabti pigment falls into two possible production groups — [PLOS ONE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7685487/); scrap copper alloys as colourant at Amara West — [Leiden record](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/access/item%3A3715923/download); failed Kos pellet attributed to iron-rich starting material — [Anal Chem](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8387973/); Roman raw materials survive synthesis — [Sci Rep](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9481618/).
- Glass: Jackson and Nicholson 2023 summarise the Qantir two-stage model and say it is unclear for Amarna — [White Rose](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/194908/1/1-s2.0-S2352409X22004564-main.pdf). Shortland's UEE overview calls the earliest workshop "a subject of debate" — [UEE](https://journalpub.escholarship.org/nelc_uee/article/3976/galley/2552/download).
- Faience: Ptolemaic faience quartz traced to Eastern Desert sources — [Materials](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9501641/).
- Gold: only an economic study (value ratios; Nubian gold access until about 1100 BCE) — [CAJ](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/406D8B078C66ACB266932D51A0E2F205/S0959774323000355a.pdf/gold-and-silver-relative-values-in-the-ancient-past.pdf).

## Sceptical and alternative pass
- Glass: Shortland (candidate) and Jackson and Nicholson both treat the earliest primary glass site as unsettled; the Qantir model is one reading. The Rehren and Pusch 2005 Science paper itself was not opened, so its claims are known only through these later sources.
- Cosmetics: Walter 1999's synthetic origin is an inference ("appear to be"), and Riesmeier 2022 widens kohl beyond lead compounds.
- Scope limits on Egyptian blue: Kos (Hellenistic) and Roman Italy papers are context, not Egyptian evidence.
- No critique of Walter 1999 or of the Egyptian-blue production model was found beyond this; nothing found on a failed replication.
